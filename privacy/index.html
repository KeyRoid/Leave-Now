Privacy Policy

Yoyu stores only timing settings required for the app to work, such as target time, travel time, buffer time, and yellow threshold. These values are stored locally on the device.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Name, email address, or other personal identity information
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos, videos, audio, camera, or microphone data
  • Payment or account information
  • Advertising identifiers

External Transfer

Yoyu does not send saved timing settings to an external server.
